<p>I went into a field of flowers, whose petals are whiter than snow and whose hearts are pure gold.</p>
<p>Far afield a woman cried, “I have killed him I loved!” and from a jar she poured blood upon the flowers whose petals are whiter than snow and whose hearts are pure gold.</p>
<p>Far afield I followed, and on the jar I read a thousand names, while from within the fresh blood bubbled to the brim.</p>
<p>“I have killed him I loved!” she cried. “The world’s athirst; now let it drink!” She passed, and far afield I watched her pouring blood upon the flowers whose petals are whiter than snow and whose hearts are pure gold.</p>
