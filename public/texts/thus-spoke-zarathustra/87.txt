<p>“To the clean are all things clean”⁠—thus say the people. I, however, say unto you: To the swine all things become swinish!</p>
<p>Therefore preach the visionaries and bowed-heads (whose hearts are also bowed down): “The world itself is a filthy monster.”</p>
<p>For these are all unclean spirits; especially those, however, who have no peace or rest, unless they see the world <em>from the backside</em>⁠—the backworldsmen!</p>
<p><em>To those</em> do I say it to the face, although it sound unpleasantly: the world resembleth man, in that it hath a backside⁠—<em>so much</em> is true!</p>
<p>There is in the world much filth: <em>so much</em> is true! But the world itself is not therefore a filthy monster!</p>
<p>There is wisdom in the fact that much in the world smelleth badly: loathing itself createth wings, and fountain-divining powers!</p>
<p>In the best there is still something to loathe; and the best is still something that must be surpassed!⁠—</p>
<p>O my brethren, there is much wisdom in the fact that much filth is in the world!⁠—</p>
