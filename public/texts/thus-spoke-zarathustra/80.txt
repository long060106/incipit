<p>To be true⁠—that <em>can</em> few be! And he who can, will not! Least of all, however, can the good be true.</p>
<p>Oh, those good ones! <em>Good men never speak the truth.</em> For the spirit, thus to be good, is a malady.</p>
<p>They yield, those good ones, they submit themselves; their heart repeateth, their soul obeyeth: <em>he</em>, however, who obeyeth, <em>doth not listen to himself</em>!</p>
<p>All that is called evil by the good, must come together in order that one truth may be born. O my brethren, are ye also evil enough for <em>this</em> truth?</p>
<p>The daring venture, the prolonged distrust, the cruel Nay, the tedium, the cutting-into-the-quick⁠—how seldom do <em>these</em> come together! Out of such seed, however⁠—is truth produced!</p>
<p><em>Beside</em> the bad conscience hath hitherto grown all <em>knowledge</em>! Break up, break up, ye discerning ones, the old tables!</p>
