<p>Ye flee from me? Ye are frightened? Ye tremble at this word?</p>
<p>O my brethren, when I enjoined you to break up the good, and the tables of the good, then only did I embark man on his high seas.</p>
<p>And now only cometh unto him the great terror, the great outlook, the great sickness, the great nausea, the great seasickness.</p>
<p>False shores and false securities did the good teach you; in the lies of the good were ye born and bred. Everything hath been radically contorted and distorted by the good.</p>
<p>But he who discovered the country of “man,” discovered also the country of “man’s future.” Now shall ye be sailors for me, brave, patient!</p>
<p>Keep yourselves up betimes, my brethren, learn to keep yourselves up! The sea stormeth: many seek to raise themselves again by you.</p>
<p>The sea stormeth: all is in the sea. Well! Cheer up! Ye old seaman-hearts!</p>
<p>What of fatherland! <em>Thither</em> striveth our helm where our <em>children’s land</em> is! Thitherwards, stormier than the sea, stormeth our great longing!⁠—</p>
