<p>It is not enough for me that the lightning no longer doeth harm. I do not wish to conduct it away: it shall learn⁠—to work for <em>me</em>.⁠—</p>
<p>My wisdom hath accumulated long like a cloud, it becometh stiller and darker. So doeth all wisdom which shall one day bear <em>lightnings</em>.⁠—</p>
<p>Unto these men of today will I not be <em>light</em>, nor be called light. <em>Them</em>⁠—will I blind: lightning of my wisdom! put out their eyes!</p>
