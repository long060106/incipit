<p>If I be a diviner and full of the divining spirit which wandereth on high mountain-ridges, ’twixt two seas⁠—</p>
<p>Wandereth ’twixt the past and the future as a heavy cloud⁠—hostile to sultry plains, and to all that is weary and can neither die nor live:</p>
<p>Ready for lightning in its dark bosom, and for the redeeming flash of light, charged with lightnings which say Yea! which laugh Yea! ready for divining flashes of lightning:⁠—</p>
<p>—Blessed, however, is he who is thus charged! And verily, long must he hang like a heavy tempest on the mountain, who shall one day kindle the light of the future!⁠—</p>
<p>Oh, how could I not be ardent for Eternity and for the marriage-ring of rings⁠—the ring of the return?</p>
<p>Never yet have I found the woman by whom I should like to have children, unless it be this woman whom I love: for I love thee, O Eternity!</p>
