<blockquote><p class="verse">—Ha! <br> Solemnly! <br> In effect solemnly! <br> A worthy beginning! <br> Afric manner, solemnly! <br> Of a lion worthy, <br> Or perhaps of a virtuous howl-monkey⁠— <br> —But it’s naught to you, <br> Ye friendly damsels dearly loved, <br> At whose own feet to me, <br> The first occasion, <br> To a European under palm-trees, <br> A seat is now granted. Selah.</p> <p class="verse">Wonderful, truly! <br> Here do I sit now, <br> The desert nigh, and yet I am <br> So far still from the desert, <br> Even in naught yet deserted: <br> That is, I’m swallowed down <br> By this the smallest oasis⁠—: <br> —It opened up just yawning, <br> Its loveliest mouth agape, <br> Most sweet-odoured of all mouthlets: <br> Then fell I right in, <br> Right down, right through⁠—in ’mong you, <br> Ye friendly damsels dearly loved! Selah.</p> <p class="verse">Hail! hail! to that whale, fishlike, <br> If it thus for its guest’s convenience <br> Made things nice!⁠—(ye well know, <br> Surely, my learned allusion?) <br> Hail to its belly, <br> If it had e’er <br> A such loveliest oasis-belly <br> As this is: though however I doubt about it, <br> —With this come I out of Old-Europe, <br> That doubt’th more eagerly than doth any <br> Elderly married woman. <br> May the Lord improve it! <br> Amen!</p> <p class="verse">Here do I sit now, <br> In this the smallest oasis, <br> Like a date indeed, <br> Brown, quite sweet, gold-suppurating, <br> For rounded mouth of maiden longing, <br> But yet still more for youthful, maidlike, <br> Ice-cold and snow-white and incisory <br> Front teeth: and for such assuredly, <br> Pine the hearts all of ardent date-fruits. Selah.</p> <p class="verse">To the there-named south-fruits now, <br> Similar, all-too-similar, <br> Do I lie here; by little <br> Flying insects <br> Round-sniffled and round-played, <br> And also by yet littler, <br> Foolisher, and peccabler <br> Wishes and fantasies⁠— <br> Environed by you, <br> Ye silent, presentientest <br> Maiden-kittens, <br> Dudu and Suleika <br> —<em>Roundsphinxed</em>, that into one word <br> I may crowd much feeling: <br> (Forgive me, O God, <br> All such speech-sinning!) <br> —Sit I here the best of air sniffling, <br> Paradisal air, truly, <br> Bright and buoyant air, golden-mottled, <br> As goodly air as ever <br> From lunar orb downfell⁠— <br> Be it by hazard, <br> Or supervened it by arrogancy? <br> As the ancient poets relate it. <br> But doubter, I’m now calling it <br> In question: with this do I come indeed <br> Out of Europe, <br> That doubt’th more eagerly than doth any <br> Elderly married woman. <br> May the Lord improve it! <br> Amen.</p> <p class="verse">This the finest air drinking, <br> With nostrils out-swelled like goblets, <br> Lacking future, lacking remembrances <br> Thus do I sit here, ye <br> Friendly damsels dearly loved, <br> And look at the palm-tree there, <br> How it, to a dance-girl, like, <br> Doth bow and bend and on its haunches bob <br> —One doth it too, when one view’th it long!⁠— <br> To a dance-girl like, who as it seem’th to me, <br> Too long, and dangerously persistent, <br> Always, always, just on <em>single</em> leg hath stood? <br> —Then forgot she thereby, as it seem’th to me, <br> The <em>other</em> leg? <br> For vainly I, at least, <br> Did search for the amissing <br> Fellow-jewel <br> —Namely, the other leg⁠— <br> In the sanctified precincts, <br> Nigh her very dearest, very tenderest, <br> Flapping and fluttering and flickering skirting. <br> Yea, if ye should, ye beauteous friendly ones, <br> Quite take my word: <br> She hath, alas! <em>lost</em> it! <br> Hu! Hu! Hu! Hu! Hu! <br> It is away! <br> Forever away! <br> The other leg! <br> Oh, pity for that loveliest other leg! <br> Where may it now tarry, all-forsaken weeping? <br> The lonesomest leg? <br> In fear perhaps before a <br> Furious, yellow, blond and curled <br> Leonine monster? Or perhaps even <br> Gnawed away, nibbled badly⁠— <br> Most wretched, woeful! woeful! nibbled badly! Selah.</p> <p class="verse">Oh, weep ye not, <br> Gentle spirits! <br> Weep ye not, ye <br> Date-fruit spirits! Milk-bosoms! <br> Ye sweetwood-heart <br> Purselets! <br> Weep ye no more, <br> Pallid Dudu! <br> Be a man, Suleika! Bold! Bold! <br> —Or else should there perhaps <br> Something strengthening, heart-strengthening, <br> Here most proper be? <br> Some inspiring text? <br> Some solemn exhortation?⁠— <br> Ha! Up now! honour! <br> Moral honour! European honour! <br> Blow again, continue, <br> Bellows-box of virtue! <br> Ha! <br> Once more thy roaring, <br> Thy moral roaring! <br> As a virtuous lion <br> Nigh the daughters of deserts roaring! <br> —For virtue’s out-howl, <br> Ye very dearest maidens, <br> Is more than every <br> European fervour, European hot-hunger! <br> And now do I stand here, <br> As European, <br> I can’t be different, God’s help to me! <br> Amen!</p></blockquote>
