<p>Such sayings did I hear pious backworldsmen speak to their consciences, and verily without wickedness or guile⁠—although there is nothing more guileful in the world, or more wicked.</p>
<p>“Let the world be as it is! Raise not a finger against it!”</p>
<p>“Let whoever will choke and stab and skin and scrape the people: raise not a finger against it! Thereby will they learn to renounce the world.”</p>
<p>“And thine own reason⁠—this shalt thou thyself stifle and choke; for it is a reason of this world⁠—thereby wilt thou learn thyself to renounce the world.”⁠—</p>
<p>—Shatter, shatter, O my brethren, those old tables of the pious! Tatter the maxims of the world-maligners!⁠—</p>
