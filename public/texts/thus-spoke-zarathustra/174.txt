<p>Have ye now learned my song? Have ye divined what it would say? Well! Cheer up! Ye higher men, sing now my roundelay!</p>
<p>Sing now yourselves the song, the name of which is “Once more,” the signification of which is “Unto all eternity!”⁠—sing, ye higher men, Zarathustra’s roundelay!</p>
<blockquote><p class="verse">O man! Take heed! <br> What saith deep midnight’s voice indeed? <br> “I slept my sleep⁠—, <br> “From deepest dream I’ve woke, and plead:⁠— <br> “The world is deep, <br> “And deeper than the day could read. <br> “Deep is its woe⁠—, <br> “Joy⁠—deeper still than grief can be: <br> “Woe saith: Hence! Go! <br> “But joys all want eternity⁠—, <br> “⁠—Want deep, profound eternity!”</p></blockquote>
