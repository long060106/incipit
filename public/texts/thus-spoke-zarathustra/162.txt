<p>And once more began Zarathustra to speak. “O my new friends,” said he⁠—“ye strange ones, ye higher men, how well do ye now please me⁠—</p>
<p>“⁠—Since ye have again become joyful! Ye have, verily, all blossomed forth: it seemeth to me that for such flowers as you, <em>new festivals</em> are required.</p>
<p>“⁠—A little valiant nonsense, some divine service and ass-festival, some old joyful Zarathustra fool, some blusterer to blow your souls bright.</p>
<p>“Forget not this night and this ass-festival, ye higher men! <em>That</em> did ye devise when with me, that do I take as a good omen⁠—such things only the convalescents devise!</p>
<p>“And should ye celebrate it again, this ass-festival, do it from love to yourselves, do it also from love to me! And in remembrance of me!”</p>
<p>Thus spake Zarathustra.</p>
