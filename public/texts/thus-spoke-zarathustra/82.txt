<p>There is an old illusion⁠—it is called good and evil. Around soothsayers and astrologers hath hitherto revolved the orbit of this illusion.</p>
<p>Once did one <em>believe</em> in soothsayers and astrologers; and <em>therefore</em> did one believe, “Everything is fate: thou shalt, for thou must!”</p>
<p>Then again did one distrust all soothsayers and astrologers; and <em>therefore</em> did one believe, “Everything is freedom: thou canst, for thou willest!”</p>
<p>O my brethren, concerning the stars and the future there hath hitherto been only illusion, and not knowledge; and <em>therefore</em> concerning good and evil there hath hitherto been only illusion and not knowledge!</p>
