<p>If ye would go up high, then use your own legs! Do not get yourselves <em>carried</em> aloft; do not seat yourselves on other people’s backs and heads!</p>
<p>Thou hast mounted, however, on horseback? Thou now ridest briskly up to thy goal? Well, my friend! But thy lame foot is also with thee on horseback!</p>
<p>When thou reachest thy goal, when thou alightest from thy horse: precisely on thy <em>height</em>, thou higher man⁠—then wilt thou stumble!</p>
