<p>Have ye courage, O my brethren? Are ye stouthearted? <em>Not</em> the courage before witnesses, but anchorite and eagle courage, which not even a God any longer beholdeth?</p>
<p>Cold souls, mules, the blind and the drunken, I do not call stouthearted. He hath heart who knoweth fear, but <em>vanquisheth</em> it; who seeth the abyss, but with <em>pride</em>.</p>
<p>He who seeth the abyss, but with eagle’s eyes⁠—he who with eagle’s talons <em>graspeth</em> the abyss: he hath courage.⁠⸺</p>
