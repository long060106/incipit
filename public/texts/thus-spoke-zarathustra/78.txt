<p>Thus wisheth the type of noble souls: they desire to have nothing <em>gratuitously</em>, least of all, life.</p>
<p>He who is of the populace wisheth to live gratuitously; we others, however, to whom life hath given itself⁠—we are ever considering <em>what</em> we can best give <em>in return</em>!</p>
<p>And verily, it is a noble dictum which saith: “What life promiseth <em>us</em>, that promise will <em>we</em> keep⁠—to life!”</p>
<p>One should not wish to enjoy where one doth not contribute to the enjoyment. And one should not <em>wish</em> to enjoy!</p>
<p>For enjoyment and innocence are the most bashful things. Neither like to be sought for. One should <em>have</em> them⁠—but one should rather <em>seek</em> for guilt and pain!⁠—</p>
