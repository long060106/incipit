<p>Roxane, The duenna, Cyrano.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> We are going to Clomire’s house.<br> (She points to the door opposite):<br> Alcandre and Lysimon are to discourse!</p>
<p class="verse">THE DUENNA (putting her little finger in her ear):<br> Yes! But my little finger tells me we shall miss them.</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> ’Twere a pity to miss such apes!</p>
<p>(They have come to Clomire’s door.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE DUENNA:<br> Oh, see! The knocker is muffled up!<br> (Speaking to the knocker):<br> So they have gagged that metal tongue of yours, little noisy one, lest it<br> should disturb the fine orators!</p>
<p>(She lifts it carefully and knocks with precaution.)</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (seeing that the door opens):<br> Let us enter!<br> (On the threshold, to Cyrano):<br> If Christian comes, as I feel sure he will, bid him wait for me!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (quickly, as she is going in):<br> Listen!<br> (She turns):<br> What mean you to question him on, as is your wont, to-night?</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Oh—</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (eagerly):<br> Well, say.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> But you will be mute?</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Mute as a fish.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> I shall not question him at all, but say: Give rein to your fancy! Prepare<br> not your speeches,—but speak the thoughts as they come! Speak to me of love,<br> and speak splendidly!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (smiling):<br> Very good!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> But secret!. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Secret.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Not a word!</p>
<p>(She enters and shuts the door.)</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (when the door is shut, bowing to her):<br> A thousand thanks!</p>
<p>(The door opens again, and Roxane puts her head out.)</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Lest he prepare himself!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> The devil!—no, no!</p>
<p class="verse">BOTH TOGETHER:<br> Secret.</p>
<p>(The door shuts.)</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (calling):<br> Christian!</p>
