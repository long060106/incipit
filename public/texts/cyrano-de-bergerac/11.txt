<p>The same. Two children, who have just trotted into the shop.</p>
<p class="verse">RAGUENEAU:<br> What would you, little ones?</p>
<p class="verse">FIRST CHILD:<br> Three pies.</p>
<p class="verse">RAGUENEAU (serving them):<br> See, hot and well browned.</p>
<p class="verse">SECOND CHILD:<br> If it please you, Sir, will you wrap them up for us?</p>
<p class="verse">RAGUENEAU (aside, distressed):<br> Alas! one of my bags!<br> (To the children):<br> What? Must I wrap them up?<br> (He takes a bag, and just as he is about to put in the pies, he reads):<br> ‘Ulysses thus, on leaving fair Penelope. . .’<br> Not that one!<br> (He puts it aside, and takes another, and as he is about to put in the pies, he reads):<br> ‘The gold-locked Phoebus. . .’<br> Nay, nor that one!. . .</p>
<p>(Same play.)</p>
<p class="verse">LISE (impatiently):<br> What are you dallying for?</p>
<p class="verse">RAGUENEAU:<br> Here! here! here<br> (He chooses a third, resignedly):<br> The sonnet to Phillis!. . .but ’tis hard to part with it!</p>
<p class="verse">LISE:<br> By good luck he has made up his mind at last!<br> (Shrugging her shoulders):<br> Nicodemus!</p>
<p>(She mounts on a chair, and begins to range plates on a dresser.)</p>
<p class="verse">RAGUENEAU (taking advantage of the moment she turns her back, calls back the children, who are already at the door):<br> Hist! children!. . .render me back the sonnet to Phillis, and you shall have six pies instead of three.</p>
<p>(The children give him back the bag, seize the cakes quickly, and go out.)</p>
<p class="verse">RAGUENEAU (smoothing out the paper, begins to declaim):<br> ‘Phillis!. . .’ On that sweet name a smear of butter! ‘Phillis!. . .’</p>
<p>(Cyrano enters hurriedly.)</p>
