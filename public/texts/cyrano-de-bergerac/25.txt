<p>Cyrano, Christian.</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> I know all that is needful. Here’s occasion<br> For you to deck yourself with glory. Come,<br> Lose no time; put away those sulky looks,<br> Come to your house with me, I’ll teach you. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> No!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Why?</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> I will wait for Roxane here.</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> How? Crazy?<br> Come quick with me and learn. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> No, no! I say.<br> I am aweary of these borrowed letters,<br> —Borrowed love-makings! Thus to act a part,<br> And tremble all the time!—’Twas well enough<br> At the beginning!—Now I know she loves!<br> I fear no longer!—I will speak myself.</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Mercy!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> And how know you I cannot speak?—<br> I am not such a fool when all is said!<br> I’ve by your lessons profited. You’ll see<br> I shall know how to speak alone! The devil!<br> I know at least to clasp her in my arms!<br> (Seeing Roxane come out from Clomire’s house):<br> —It is she! Cyrano, no!—Leave me not!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (bowing):<br> Speak for yourself, my friend, and take your chance.</p>
<p>(He disappears behind the garden wall.)</p>
