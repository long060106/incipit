<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Ah no! young blade! <p>(Tableau. A circle of curious spectators in the pit; the marquises and officers mingled with the common people; the pages climbing on each other’s shoulders to see better. All the women standing up in the boxes. To the right, De Guiche and his retinue. Left, Le Bret, Ragueneau, Cyrano, etc.)</p>

<p class="verse">Oh, for a rhyme, a rhyme in o?—<br> You wriggle, starch-white, my eel?<br> A rhyme! a rhyme! The white feather you SHOW!<br> Tac! <p class="verse">JODELET (who has looked out):<br> They hoot Montfleury!</p>
<p class="verse">BELLEROSE (solemnly):<br> Sic transit!. . .<br> (To the porters):<br> Sweep—close all, but leave the lights.<br> We sup, but later on we must return,<br> For a rehearsal of to-morrow’s farce.</p>
<p>(Jodelet and Bellerose go out, bowing low to Cyrano.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE PORTER (to Cyrano):<br> You do not dine, Sir?</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> No.</p>
<p>(The porter goes out.)</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET:<br> Because?</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (proudly):<br> Because. . .<br> (Changing his tone as the porter goes away):<br> I have no money!. . .</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET (with the action of throwing a bag):<br> How! The bag of crowns?. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Paternal bounty, in a day, thou’rt sped!</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET:<br> How live the next month?. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> I have nothing left.</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET:<br> Folly!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> But what a graceful action! Think!</p>
<p class="verse">THE BUFFET-GIRL (coughing, behind her counter):<br> Hum!<br> (Cyrano and Le Bret turn. She comes timidly forward):<br> Sir, my heart mislikes to know you fast.<br> (Showing the buffet):<br> See, all you need. Serve yourself!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (taking off his hat):<br> Gentle child,<br> Although my Gascon pride would else forbid<br> To take the least bestowal from your hands,<br> My fear of wounding you outweighs that pride,<br> And bids accept. . .<br> (He goes to the buffet):<br> A trifle!. . .These few grapes.<br> (She offers him the whole bunch. He takes a few):<br> Nay, but this bunch!. . .<br> (She tries to give him wine, but he stops her):<br> A glass of water fair!. . .<br> And half a macaroon!</p>
<p>(He gives back the other half.)</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET:<br> What foolery!</p>
<p class="verse">THE BUFFET-GIRL:<br> Take something else!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> I take your hand to kiss.</p>
<p>(He kisses her hand as though she were a princess.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE BUFFET-GIRL:<br> Thank you, kind Sir!<br> (She courtesies):<br> Good-night.</p>
<p>(She goes out.)</p>
