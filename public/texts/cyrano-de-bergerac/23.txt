<p>Roxane, De Guiche, the duenna standing a little way off.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (courtesying to De Guiche):<br> I was going out.</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> I come to take my leave.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Whither go you?</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> To the war.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Ah!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Ay, to-night.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Oh!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> I am ordered away. We are to besiege Arras.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Ah—to besiege?. . .</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Ay. My going moves you not, meseems.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Nay. . .</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> I am grieved to the core of the heart. Shall I again behold you?. . .When?<br> I know not. Heard you that I am named commander?. . .</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (indifferently):<br> Bravo!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Of the Guards regiment.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (startled):<br> What! the Guards?</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Ay, where serves your cousin, the swaggering boaster. I will find a way to<br> revenge myself on him at Arras.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (choking):<br> What mean you? The Guards go to Arras?</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (laughing):<br> Bethink you, is it not my own regiment?</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (falling seated on the bench—aside):<br> Christian!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> What ails you?</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (moved deeply):<br> Oh—I am in despair! The man one loves!—at the war!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (surprised and delighted):<br> You say such sweet words to me! ’Tis the first time!—and just when I must<br> quit you!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (collected, and fanning herself):<br> Thus,—you would fain revenge your grudge against my cousin?</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> My fair lady is on his side?</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Nay,—against him!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Do you see him often?</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> But very rarely.</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> He is ever to be met now in company with one of the cadets,. . .one New—<br> villen—viller—</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Of high stature?</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Fair-haired!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Ay, a red-headed fellow!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Handsome!. . .</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Tut!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> But dull-witted.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> One would think so, to look at him!<br> (Changing her tone):<br> How mean you to play your revenge on Cyrano? Perchance you think to put him<br> i’ the thick of the shots? Nay, believe me, that were a poor vengeance—he<br> would love such a post better than aught else! I know the way to wound his<br> pride far more keenly!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> What then? Tell. . .</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> If, when the regiment march to Arras, he were left here with his beloved<br> boon companions, the Cadets, to sit with crossed arms so long as the war<br> lasted! There is your method, would you enrage a man of his kind; cheat him<br> of his chance of mortal danger, and you punish him right fiercely.</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (coming nearer):<br> O woman! woman! Who but a woman had e’er devised so subtle a trick?</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> See you not how he will eat out his heart, while his friends gnaw their<br> thick fists for that they are deprived of the battle? So are you best<br> avenged.</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> You love me, then, a little?<br> (She smiles):<br> I would fain—seeing you thus espouse my cause, Roxane—believe it a proof<br> of love!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> ’Tis a proof of love!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (showing some sealed papers):<br> Here are the marching orders; they will be sent instantly to each company—<br> except—<br> (He detaches one):<br> —This one! ’Tis that of the Cadets.<br> (He puts it in his pocket):<br> This I keep.<br> (Laughing):<br> Ha! ha! ha! Cyrano! His love of battle!. . .So you can play tricks on<br> people?. . .you, of all ladies!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Sometimes!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (coming close to her):<br> Oh! how I love you!—to distraction! Listen! To-night—true, I ought to<br> start—but—how leave you now that I feel your heart is touched! Hard by, in<br> the Rue d’Orleans, is a convent founded by Father Athanasius, the syndic of<br> the Capuchins. True that no layman may enter—but—I can settle that with the<br> good Fathers! Their habit sleeves are wide enough to hide me in. ’Tis they<br> who serve Richelieu’s private chapel: and from respect to the uncle, fear the<br> nephew. All will deem me gone. I will come to you, masked. Give me leave to<br> wait till tomorrow, sweet Lady Fanciful!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> But, of this be rumored, your glory. . .</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Bah!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> But the siege—Arras. . .</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> ’Twill take its chance. Grant but permission.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> No!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Give me leave!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (tenderly):<br> It were my duty to forbid you!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Ah!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> You must go!<br> (Aside):<br> Christian stays here.<br> (Aloud):<br> I would have you heroic—Antoine!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> O heavenly word! You love, then, him?. . .</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> . . .For whom I trembled.</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (in an ecstasy):<br> Ah! I go then!<br> (He kisses her hand):<br> Are you content?</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Yes, my friend!</p>
<p>(He goes out.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE DUENNA (making behind his back a mocking courtesy):<br> Yes, my friend!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (to the duenna):<br> Not a word of what I have done. Cyrano would never pardon me for stealing<br> his fighting from him!<br> (She calls toward the house):<br> Cousin!</p>
