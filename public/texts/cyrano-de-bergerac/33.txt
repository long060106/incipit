<p>The same. Roxane, Christian, the friar, Ragueneau, lackeys, the duenna.</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (to Roxane):<br> You?<br> (Recognizing Christian, in amazement):<br> He?<br> (Bowing, with admiration, to Roxane):<br> Cunningly contrived!<br> (To Cyrano):<br> My compliments—Sir Apparatus-maker!<br> Your story would arrest at Peter’s gate<br> Saints eager for their Paradise! Note well<br> The details. ’Faith! They’d make a stirring book!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (bowing):<br> I shall not fail to follow your advice.</p>
<p class="verse">THE FRIAR (showing with satisfaction the two lovers to De Guiche):<br> A handsome couple, son, made one by you!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (with a freezing look):<br> Ay!<br> (To Roxane):<br> Bid your bridegroom, Madame, fond farewell.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> Why so?</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (to Christian):<br> Even now the regiment departs.<br> Join it!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> It goes to battle?</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Without doubt.</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> But the Cadets go not?</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> Oh ay! they go.<br> (Drawing out the paper he had put in his pocket):<br> Here is the order.<br> (To Christian):<br> Baron, bear it, quick!<br> <br> ROXANE (throwing herself in Christian’s arms):<br> Christian!</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE (sneeringly to Cyrano):<br> The wedding-night is far, methinks!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (aside):<br> He thinks to give me pain of death by this!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (to Roxane):<br> Oh! once again! Your lips!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Come, come, enough!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (still kissing Roxane):<br> —’Tis hard to leave her, you know not. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (trying to draw him away):<br> I know.</p>
<p>(Sound of drums beating a march in the distance.)</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> The regiment starts!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (To Cyrano, holding back Christian, whom Cyrano is drawing away):<br> Oh!—I trust him you!<br> Promise me that no risks shall put his life<br> In danger!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> I will try my best, but promise. . .<br> That I cannot!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> But swear he shall be prudent?</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Again, I’ll do my best, but. . .</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> In the siege<br> Let him not suffer!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> All that man can do,<br> I. . .</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> That he shall be faithful!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Doubtless, but. . .</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE:<br> That he will write oft?</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (pausing):<br> That, I promise you!</p>
<p>Curtain.</p>
