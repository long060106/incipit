<p>The same, all but Lignière. De Guiche, Valvert, then Montfleury.</p>
<p class="verse">A marquis (watching De Guiche, who comes down from Roxane’s box, and crosses the pit surrounded by obsequious noblemen, among them the Viscount de Valvert):<br> He pays a fine court, your De Guiche!</p>
<p class="verse">ANOTHER:<br> Faugh!. . .Another Gascon!</p>
<p class="verse">THE FIRST:<br> Ay, but the cold, supple Gascon—that is the stuff success is made of!<br> Believe me, we had best make our bow to him.</p>
<p>(They go toward De Guiche.)</p>
<p class="verse">SECOND MARQUIS:<br> What fine ribbons! How call you the color, Count de Guiche? ‘Kiss me, my<br> darling,’ or ‘Timid Fawn?’</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> ’Tis the color called ‘Sick Spaniard.’</p>
<p class="verse">FIRST MARQUIS:<br> ’Faith! The color speaks truth, for, thanks to your valor, things will soon<br> go ill for Spain in Flanders.</p>
<p class="verse">DE GUICHE:<br> I go on the stage! Will you come?<br> (He goes toward the stage, followed by the marquises and gentlemen. Turning, he calls):<br> Come you Valvert!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (who is watching and listening, starts on hearing this name):<br> The Viscount! Ah! I will throw full in his face my. . .<br> (He puts his hand in his pocket, and finds there the hand of a pickpocket who is about to rob him. He turns round):<br> Hey?</p>
<p class="verse">THE PICKPOCKET:<br> Oh!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (holding him tightly):<br> I was looking for a glove.</p>
<p class="verse">THE PICKPOCKET (smiling piteously):<br> And you find a hand.<br> (Changing his tone, quickly and in a whisper):<br> Let me but go, and I will deliver you a secret.</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (still holding him):<br> What is it?</p>
<p class="verse">THE PICKPOCKET:<br> Lignière. . .he who has just left you. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (same play):<br> Well?</p>
<p class="verse">THE PICKPOCKET:<br> His life is in peril. A song writ by him has given offense in high places—<br> and a hundred men—I am of them—are posted to-night. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> A hundred men! By whom posted?</p>
<p class="verse">THE PICKPOCKET:<br> I may not say—a secret. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (shrugging his shoulders):<br> Oh!</p>
<p class="verse">THE PICKPOCKET (with great dignity):<br> . . .Of the profession.</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Where are they posted?</p>
<p class="verse">THE PICKPOCKET:<br> At the Porte de Nesle. On his way homeward. Warn him.</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (letting go of his wrists):<br> But where can I find him?</p>
<p class="verse">THE PICKPOCKET:<br> Run round to all the taverns—The Golden Wine Press, the Pine Cone, The Belt<br> that Bursts, The Two Torches, The Three Funnels, and at each leave a word that<br> shall put him on his guard.</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Good—I fly! Ah, the scoundrels! A hundred men ’gainst one!<br> (Looking lovingly at Roxane):<br> Ah, to leave her!. . .<br> (looking with rage at Valvert):<br> and him!. . .But save Lignière I must!</p>
<p>(He hurries out. De Guiche, the viscount, the marquises, have all disappeared behind the curtain to take their places on the benches placed on the stage. The pit is quite full; the galleries and boxes are also crowded.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE AUDIENCE:<br> Begin!</p>
<p class="verse">A BURGHER (whose wig is drawn up on the end of a string by a page in the upper gallery):<br> My wig!</p>
<p class="verse">CRIES OF DELIGHT:<br> He is bald! Bravo, pages—ha! ha! ha!. . .</p>
<p class="verse">THE BURGHER (furious, shaking his fist):<br> Young villain!</p>
<p class="verse">LAUGHTER AND CRIES (beginning very loud, and dying gradually away):<br> Ha! ha! ha! ha! ha! ha!</p>
<p>(Total silence.)</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET (astonished):<br> What means this sudden silence?. . .<br> (A spectator says something to him in a low voice):<br> Is’t true?</p>
<p class="verse">THE SPECTATOR:<br> I have just heard it on good authority.</p>
<p class="verse">MURMURS (spreading through the hall):<br> Hush! Is it he? No! Ay, I say!<br> In the box with the bars in front!<br> The Cardinal! The Cardinal! The Cardinal!</p>
<p class="verse">A PAGE:<br> The devil! We shall have to behave ourselves. . .</p>
<p>(A knock is heard upon the stage. Every one is motionless. A pause.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE VOICE OF A MARQUIS (in the silence, behind the curtain):<br> Snuff that candle!</p>
<p class="verse">ANOTHER MARQUIS (putting his head through the opening in the curtain):<br> A chair!</p>
<p>(A chair is passed from hand to hand, over the heads of the spectators. The marquis takes it and disappears, after blowing some kisses to the boxes.)</p>
<p class="verse">A SPECTATOR:<br> Silence!</p>
<p>(Three knocks are heard on the stage. The curtain opens in the centre Tableau. The marquises in insolent attitudes seated on each side of the stage. The scene represents a pastoral landscape. Four little lusters light the stage; the violins play softly.)</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET (in a low voice to Ragueneau):<br> Montfleury comes on the scene?</p>
<p class="verse">RAGUENEAU (also in a low voice):<br> Ay, ’tis he who begins.</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET:<br> Cyrano is not here.</p>
<p class="verse">RAGUENEAU:<br> I have lost my wager.</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET:<br> ’Tis all the better!</p>
<p>(An air on the drone-pipes is heard, and Montfleury enters, enormously stout, in an Arcadian shepherd’s dress, a hat wreathed with roses drooping over one ear, blowing into a ribboned drone pipe.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE PIT (applauding):<br> Bravo, Montfleury! Montfleury!</p>
<p class="verse">MONTFLEURY (after bowing low, begins the part of Phedon):<br> ‘Heureux qui loin des cours, dans un lieu solitaire,<br> Se prescrit a soi-meme un exil volontaire,<br> Et qui, lorsque Zephire a souffle sur les bois. . .’</p>
<p class="verse">A VOICE (from the middle of the pit):<br> Villain! Did I not forbid you to show your face here for month?</p>
<p>(General stupor. Every one turns round. Murmurs.)</p>
<p class="verse">DIFFERENT VOICES:<br> Hey?—What?—What is’t?. . .</p>
<p>(The people stand up in the boxes to look.)</p>
<p class="verse">CUIGY:<br> ’Tis he!</p>
<p class="verse">LE BRET (terrified):<br> Cyrano!</p>
<p class="verse">THE VOICE:<br> King of clowns! Leave the stage this instant!</p>
<p class="verse">ALL THE AUDIENCE (indignantly):<br> Oh!</p>
<p class="verse">MONTFLEURY:<br> But. . .</p>
<p class="verse">THE VOICE:<br> Do you dare defy me?</p>
<p class="verse">DIFFERENT VOICES (from the pit and the boxes):<br> Peace! Enough!—Play on, Montfleury—fear nothing!</p>
<p class="verse">MONTFLEURY (in a trembling voice):<br> ‘Heureux qui loin des cours, dans un lieu sol—’</p>
<p class="verse">THE VOICE (more fiercely):<br> Well! Chief of all the blackguards, must I come and give you a taste of my cane?</p>
<p>(A hand holding a cane starts up over the heads of the spectators.)</p>
<p class="verse">MONTFLEURY (in a voice that trembles more and more):<br> ‘Heureux qui. . .’</p>
<p>(The cane is shaken.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE VOICE:<br> Off the stage!</p>
<p class="verse">THE PIT:<br> Oh!</p>
<p class="verse">MONTFLEURY (choking):<br> ‘Heureux qui loin des cours. . .’</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (appearing suddenly in the pit, standing on a chair, his arms crossed, his beaver cocked fiercely, his mustache bristling, his nose terrible to see):<br> Ah! I shall be angry in a minute!. . .</p>
<p>(Sensation.)</p>
