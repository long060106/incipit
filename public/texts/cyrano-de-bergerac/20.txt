<p>Cyrano, Christian, the Gascons, the musketeer, Lise.</p>
<p class="verse">A CADET (half opening the door):<br> Naught here!. . .The silence of the grave!<br> I dare not look. . .<br> (He puts his head in):<br> Why?. . .</p>
<p class="verse">ALL THE CADETS (entering, and seeing Cyrano and Christian embracing):<br> Oh!. . .</p>
<p class="verse">A CADET:<br> This passes all!</p>
<p>(Consternation.)</p>
<p class="verse">THE MUSKETEER (mockingly):<br> Ho, ho!. . .</p>
<p class="verse">CARBON:<br> Our demon has become a saint?<br> Struck on one nostril—lo! he turns the other!</p>
<p class="verse">MUSKETEER:<br> Then we may speak about his nose, henceforth!. . .<br> (Calling to Lise, boastfully):<br> —Ah, Lise, see here!<br> (Sniffing ostentatiously):<br> O heavens!. . .what a stink!. . .<br> (Going up to Cyrano):<br> You, sir, without a doubt have sniffed it up!<br> —What is the smell I notice here?</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (cuffing his head):<br> Clove-heads.</p>
<p>(General delight. The cadets have found the old Cyrano again! They turn somersaults.)</p>
<p>Curtain.</p>
