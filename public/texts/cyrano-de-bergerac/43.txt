<p>Christian, Cyrano. At back Roxane talking to Carbon and some cadets.</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN (calling toward Cyrano’s tent):<br> Cyrano!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO (reappearing, fully armed):<br> What? Why so pale?</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> She does not love me!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> What?</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> ’Tis you she loves!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> No!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> —For she loves me only for my soul!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Truly?</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Yes! Thus—you see, that soul is you,. . .<br> Therefore, ’tis you she loves!—And you—love her!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> I?</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Oh, I know it!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Ay, ’tis true!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> You love<br> To madness!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Ay! and worse!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Then tell her so!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> No!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> And why not?</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Look at my face!—be answered!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> She’d love me—were I ugly.</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Said she so?</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Ay! in those words!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> I’m glad she told you that!<br> But pooh!—believe it not! I am well pleased<br> She thought to tell you. Take it not for truth.<br> Never grow ugly:—she’d reproach me then!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> That I intend discovering!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> No! I beg!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Ay! she shall choose between us!—Tell her all!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> No! no! I will not have it! Spare me this!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Because my face is haply fair, shall I<br> Destroy your happiness? ’Twere too unjust!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> And I,—because by Nature’s freak I have<br> The gift to say—all that perchance you feel.<br> Shall I be fatal to your happiness?</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Tell all!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> It is ill done to tempt me thus!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Too long I’ve borne about within myself<br> A rival to myself—I’ll make an end!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> Christian!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Our union, without witness—secret—<br> Clandestine—can be easily dissolved<br> If we survive.</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> My God!—he still persists!</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> I will be loved myself—or not at all!<br> —I’ll go see what they do—there, at the end<br> Of the post: speak to her, and then let her choose<br> One of us two!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> It will be you.</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Pray God!<br> (He calls):<br> Roxane!</p>
<p class="verse">CYRANO:<br> No! no!</p>
<p class="verse">ROXANE (coming up quickly):<br> What?</p>
<p class="verse">CHRISTIAN:<br> Cyrano has things<br> Important for your ear. . .</p>
<p>(She hastens to Cyrano. Christian goes out.)</p>
