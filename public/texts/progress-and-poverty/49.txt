<blockquote><p class="verse">Why hesitate? Ye are full-bearded men, <br> With God-implanted will, and courage if <br> Ye dare but show it. Never yet was will <br> But found some way or means to work it out, <br> Nor e’er did Fortune frown on him who dared. <br> Shall we in presence of this grievous wrong, <br> In this supremest moment of all time, <br> Stand trembling, cowering, when with one bold stroke <br> These groaning millions might be ever free?⁠— <br> And that one stroke so just, so greatly good, <br> So level with the happiness of man, <br> That all the angels will applaud the deed.</p> <i>E. R. Taylor</i></blockquote>
