<p class="verse">Of fame or life, <br> Which do you hold more dear? <br> Of life or wealth, <br> To which would you adhere? <br> Keep life and lose those other things; <br> Keep them and lose your life:⁠—which brings <br> Sorrow and pain more near?</p>
<p class="verse">Thus we may see, <br> Who cleaves to fame <br> Rejects what is more great; <br> Who loves large stores <br> Gives up the richer state.</p>
<p class="verse">Who is content <br> Needs fear no shame. <br> Who knows to stop <br> Incurs no blame. <br> From danger free <br> Long live shall he.<sup>80</sup></p>
