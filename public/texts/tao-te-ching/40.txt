<p>When one is about to take an inspiration, he is sure to make a (previous) expiration; when he is going to weaken another, he will first strengthen him; when he is going to overthrow another, he will first have raised him up; when he is going to despoil another, he will first have made gifts to him:⁠—this is called “Hiding the light (of his procedure).”</p>
<p>The soft overcomes the hard; and the weak the strong.</p>
