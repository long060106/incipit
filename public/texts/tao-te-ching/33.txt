<p>If anyone should wish to get the kingdom for himself, and to effect this by what he does, I see that he will not succeed. The kingdom is a spirit-like thing, and cannot be got by active doing. He who would so win it destroys it; he who would hold it in his grasp loses it.</p>
<p class="verse">The course and nature of things is such that <br> What was in front is now behind; <br> What warmed anon we freezing find. <br> Strength is of weakness oft the spoil; <br> The store in ruins mocks our toil.</p>
<p>Hence the sage puts away excessive effort, extravagance, and easy indulgence.<sup>65</sup></p>
