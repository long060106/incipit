<p>Heaven and earth do not act from (the impulse of) any wish to be benevolent; they deal with all things as the dogs of grass are dealt with. The sages do not act from (any wish to be) benevolent; they deal with the people as the dogs of grass are dealt with.</p>
<p>May not the space between heaven and earth be compared to a bellows?</p>
<p class="verse">’Tis emptied, yet it loses not its power; <br> ’Tis moved again, and sends forth air the more. <br> Much speech to swift exhaustion lead we see; <br> Your inner being guard, and keep it free.<sup>41</sup></p>
