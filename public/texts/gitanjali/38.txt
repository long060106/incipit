<p>This is my prayer to thee, my lord⁠—strike, strike at the root of penury in my heart.</p>
<p>Give me the strength lightly to bear my joys and sorrows.</p>
<p>Give me the strength to make my love fruitful in service.</p>
<p>Give me the strength never to disown the poor or bend my knees before insolent might.</p>
<p>Give me the strength to raise my mind high above daily trifles.</p>
<p>And give me the strength to surrender my strength to thy will with love.</p>
