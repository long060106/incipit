<p>This is my delight, thus to wait and watch at the wayside where shadow chases light and the rain comes in the wake of the summer.</p>
<p>Messengers, with tidings from unknown skies, greet me and speed along the road. My heart is glad within, and the breath of the passing breeze is sweet.</p>
<p>From dawn till dusk I sit here before my door, and I know that of a sudden the happy moment will arrive when I shall see.</p>
<p>In the meanwhile I smile and I sing all alone. In the meanwhile the air is filling with the perfume of promise.</p>
