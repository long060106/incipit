<p>I ask for a moment’s indulgence to sit by thy side. The works that I have in hand I will finish afterwards.</p>
<p>Away from the sight of thy face my heart knows no rest nor respite, and my work becomes an endless toil in a shoreless sea of toil.</p>
<p>Today the summer has come at my window with its sighs and murmurs; and the bees are plying their minstrelsy at the court of the flowering grove.</p>
<p>Now it is time to sit quiet, face to face with thee, and to sing dedication of life in this silent and overflowing leisure.</p>
