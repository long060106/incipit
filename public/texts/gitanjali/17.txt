<p>I am here to sing thee songs. In this hall of thine I have a corner seat.</p>
<p>In thy world I have no work to do; my useless life can only break out in tunes without a purpose.</p>
<p>When the hour strikes for thy silent worship at the dark temple of midnight, command me, my master, to stand before thee to sing.</p>
<p>When in the morning air the golden harp is tuned, honour me, commanding my presence.</p>
