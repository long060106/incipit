<p>When thou commandest me to sing it seems that my heart would break with pride; and I look to thy face, and tears come to my eyes.</p>
<p>All that is harsh and dissonant in my life melts into one sweet harmony⁠—and my adoration spreads wings like a glad bird on its flight across the sea.</p>
<p>I know thou takest pleasure in my singing. I know that only as a singer I come before thy presence.</p>
<p>I touch by the edge of the far-spreading wing of my song thy feet which I could never aspire to reach.</p>
<p>Drunk with the joy of singing I forget myself and call thee friend who art my lord.</p>
