<p>It is the pang of separation that spreads throughout the world and gives birth to shapes innumerable in the infinite sky.</p>
<p>It is this sorrow of separation that gazes in silence all night from star to star and becomes lyric among rustling leaves in rainy darkness of July.</p>
<p>It is this overspreading pain that deepens into loves and desires, into sufferings and joys in human homes; and this it is that ever melts and flows in songs through my poet’s heart.</p>
