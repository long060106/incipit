<p>That I want thee, only thee⁠—let my heart repeat without end. All desires that distract me, day and night, are false and empty to the core.</p>
<p>As the night keeps hidden in its gloom the petition for light, even thus in the depth of my unconsciousness rings the cry⁠—I want thee, only thee.</p>
<p>As the storm still seeks its end in peace when it strikes against peace with all its might, even thus my rebellion strikes against thy love and still its cry is⁠—I want thee, only thee.</p>
