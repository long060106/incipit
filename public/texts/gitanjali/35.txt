<p>When it was day they came into my house and said, “We shall only take the smallest room here.”</p>
<p>They said, “We shall help you in the worship of your God and humbly accept only our own share in his grace”; and then they took their seat in a corner and they sat quiet and meek.</p>
<p>But in the darkness of night I find they break into my sacred shrine, strong and turbulent, and snatch with unholy greed the offerings from God’s altar.</p>
