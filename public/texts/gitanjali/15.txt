<p>The song that I came to sing remains unsung to this day.</p>
<p>I have spent my days in stringing and in unstringing my instrument.</p>
<p>The time has not come true, the words have not been rightly set; only there is the agony of wishing in my heart.</p>
<p>The blossom has not opened; only the wind is sighing by.</p>
<p>I have not seen his face, nor have I listened to his voice; only I have heard his gentle footsteps from the road before my house.</p>
<p>The livelong day has passed in spreading his seat on the floor; but the lamp has not been lit and I cannot ask him into my house.</p>
<p>I live in the hope of meeting with him; but this meeting is not yet.</p>
