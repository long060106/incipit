<p>At this time of my parting, wish me good luck, my friends! The sky is flushed with the dawn and my path lies beautiful.</p>
<p>Ask not what I have with me to take there. I start on my journey with empty hands and expectant heart.</p>
<p>I shall put on my wedding garland. Mine is not the red-brown dress of the traveller, and though there are dangers on the way I have no fear in mind.</p>
<p>The evening star will come out when my voyage is done and the plaintive notes of the twilight melodies be struck up from the King’s gateway.</p>
