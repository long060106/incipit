<p>Thou hast made me endless, such is thy pleasure. This frail vessel thou emptiest again and again, and fillest it ever with fresh life.</p>
<p>This little flute of a reed thou hast carried over hills and dales, and hast breathed through it melodies eternally new.</p>
<p>At the immortal touch of thy hands my little heart loses its limits in joy and gives birth to utterance ineffable.</p>
<p>Thy infinite gifts come to me only on these very small hands of mine. Ages pass, and still thou pourest, and still there is room to fill.</p>
