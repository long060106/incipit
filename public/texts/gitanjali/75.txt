<p>Deliverance is not for me in renunciation. I feel the embrace of freedom in a thousand bonds of delight.</p>
<p>Thou ever pourest for me the fresh draught of thy wine of various colours and fragrance, filling this earthen vessel to the brim.</p>
<p>My world will light its hundred different lamps with thy flame and place them before the altar of thy temple.</p>
<p>No, I will never shut the doors of my senses. The delights of sight and hearing and touch will bear thy delight.</p>
<p>Yes, all my illusions will burn into illumination of joy, and all my desires ripen into fruits of love.</p>
