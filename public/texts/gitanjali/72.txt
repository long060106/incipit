<p>Is it beyond thee to be glad with the gladness of this rhythm? to be tossed and lost and broken in the whirl of this fearful joy?</p>
<p>All things rush on, they stop not, they look not behind, no power can hold them back, they rush on.</p>
<p>Keeping steps with that restless, rapid music, seasons come dancing and pass away⁠—colours, tunes, and perfumes pour in endless cascades in the abounding joy that scatters and gives up and dies every moment.</p>
