<p class="verse">FROM A COTTON PLANTATION NEAR THE RED RIVER,<br> IN LOUISIANA.</p>

<p class="verse">AUBURN:<br> DERBY AND MILLER.</p>
<p class="verse">BUFFALO:<br> DERBY, ORTON AND MULLIGAN.</p>

<p class="verse">Entered according to Act of Congress, in the year one thousand eight hundred and<br> fifty-three, by<br> Derby and Miller,<br> In the Clerk's Office of the District Court of the Northern District of New-York.</p>

<p class="verse">TO<br> HARRIET BEECHER STOWE:<br> WHOSE NAME,<br> THROUGHOUT THE WORLD, IS IDENTIFIED WITH THE<br> GREAT REFORM:<br> THIS NARRATIVE, AFFORDING ANOTHER<br> <b>Key to Uncle Tom's Cabin,</b><br> IS RESPECTFULLY DEDICATED</p>
<p>"Such dupes are men to custom, and so prone</p>
<p>To reverence what is ancient, and can plead</p>
<p>A course of long observance for its use,</p>
<p>That even servitude, the worst of ills,</p>
<p>Because delivered down from sire to son,</p>
<p>Is kept and guarded as a sacred thing.</p>
<p>But is it fit, or can it bear the shock</p>
<p>Of rational discussion, that a man</p>
<p>Compounded and made up, like other men,</p>
<p>Of elements tumultuous, in whom lust</p>
<p>And folly in as ample measure meet,</p>
<p>As in the bosom of the slave he rules,</p>
<p>Should be a despot absolute, and boast</p>
<p>Himself the only freeman of his land?"</p>
<p>Cowper.</p>
