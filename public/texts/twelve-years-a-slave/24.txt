<p>A REFRAIN OF THE RED RIVER PLANTATION.</p>
<p>"Harper's creek and roarin' ribber,</p>
<p>Thar, my dear, we'll live forebber;</p>
<p>Den we'll go to de Ingin nation,</p>
<p>All I want in dis creation,</p>
<p>Is pretty little wife and big plantation.</p>
<p>CHORUS.</p>
<p>Up dat oak and down dat ribber,</p>
<p>Two overseers and one little nigger."</p>
