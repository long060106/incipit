<p class="lede">St. Bernard points out the saints in the White Rose.</p>
<p class="verse">Absorbed in his delight, that contemplator<sup>2057</sup> <br> Assumed the willing office of a teacher, <br> And gave beginning to these holy words: <br> “The wound that Mary closed up and anointed, <br> She at her feet who is so beautiful,<sup>2058</sup> <br> She is the one who opened it and pierced it. <br> Within that order which the third seats make <br> Is seated Rachel, lower than the other,<sup>2059</sup> <br> With Beatrice, in manner as thou seest. <br> Sarah, Rebecca, Judith, and her who was <br> Ancestress of the Singer, who for dole<sup>2060</sup> <br> Of the misdeed said, ‘<i>Miserere mei</i>,’<sup>2061</sup> <br> Canst thou behold from seat to seat descending <br> Down in gradation, as with each one’s name <br> I through the Rose go down from leaf to leaf. <br> And downward from the seventh row, even as <br> Above the same, succeed the Hebrew women, <br> Dividing all the tresses of the flower; <br> Because, according to the view which Faith <br> In Christ had taken, these are the partition <br> By which the sacred stairways are divided. <br> Upon this side, where perfect is the flower <br> With each one of its petals, seated are <br> Those who believed in Christ who was to come.<sup>2062</sup> <br> Upon the other side, where intersected <br> With vacant spaces are the semicircles, <br> Are those who looked to Christ already come.<sup>2063</sup> <br> And as, upon this side, the glorious seat <br> Of the Lady of Heaven, and the other seats <br> Below it, such a great division make, <br> So opposite doth that of the great John,<sup>2064</sup> <br> Who, ever holy, desert and martyrdom <br> Endured, and afterwards two years in Hell. <br> And under him thus to divide were chosen <br> Francis, and Benedict, and Augustine, <br> And down to us the rest from round to round. <br> Behold now the high providence divine; <br> For one and other aspect of the Faith <br> In equal measure shall this garden fill. <br> And know that downward from that rank which cleaves<sup>2065</sup> <br> Midway the sequence of the two divisions, <br> Not by their proper merit are they seated; <br> But by another’s under fixed conditions;<sup>2066</sup> <br> For these are spirits one and all assoiled <br> Before they any true election had. <br> Well canst thou recognise it in their faces, <br> And also in their voices puerile, <br> If thou regard them well and hearken to them. <br> Now doubtest thou, and doubting thou art silent; <br> But I will loosen for thee the strong bond <br> In which thy subtle fancies hold thee fast. <br> Within the amplitude of this domain <br> No casual point can possibly find place, <br> No more than sadness can, or thirst, or hunger; <br> For by eternal law has been established <br> Whatever thou beholdest, so that closely <br> The ring is fitted to the finger here. <br> And therefore are these people, festinate<sup>2067</sup> <br> Unto true life, not <i>sine causa</i> here <br> More and less excellent among themselves. <br> The King, by means of whom this realm reposes <br> In so great love and in so great delight <br> That no will ventureth to ask for more, <br> In his own joyous aspect every mind <br> Creating, at his pleasure dowers with grace <br> Diversely; and let here the effect suffice. <br> And this is clearly and expressly noted <br> For you in Holy Scripture, in those twins<sup>2068</sup> <br> Who in their mother had their anger roused. <br> According to the color of the hair,<sup>2069</sup> <br> Therefore, with such a grace the light supreme <br> Consenteth that they worthily be crowned. <br> Without, then, any merit of their deeds, <br> Stationed are they in different gradations, <br> Differing only in their first acuteness.<sup>2070</sup> <br> ’Tis true that in the early centuries,<sup>2071</sup> <br> With innocence, to work out their salvation <br> Sufficient was the faith of parents only. <br> After the earlier ages were completed,<sup>2072</sup> <br> Behoved it that the males by circumcision <br> Unto their innocent wings should virtue add; <br> But after that the time of grace had come <br> Without the baptism absolute of Christ, <br> Such innocence below there was retained. <br> Look now into the face that unto Christ<sup>2073</sup> <br> Hath most resemblance; for its brightness only <br> Is able to prepare thee to see Christ.” <br> On her did I behold so great a gladness <br> Rain down, borne onward in the holy minds <br> Created through that altitude to fly, <br> That whatsoever I had seen before <br> Did not suspend me in such admiration, <br> Nor show me such similitude of God. <br> And the same Love that first descended there,<sup>2074</sup> <br> “<i>Ave Maria, gratia plena</i>,” singing, <br> In front of her his wings expanded wide. <br> Unto the canticle divine responded <br> From every part the court beatified, <br> So that each sight became serener for it.<sup>2075</sup> <br> “O holy father, who for me endurest <br> To be below here, leaving the sweet place <br> In which thou sittest by eternal lot, <br> Who is the Angel that with so much joy <br> Into the eyes is looking of our Queen, <br> Enamoured so that he seems made of fire?” <br> Thus I again recourse had to the teaching <br> Of that one who delighted him in Mary<sup>2076</sup> <br> As doth the star of morning in the sun.<sup>2077</sup> <br> And he to me: “Such gallantry and grace <br> As there can be in Angel and in soul, <br> All is in him; and thus we fain would have it; <br> Because he is the one who bore the palm <br> Down unto Mary, when the Son of God <br> To take our burden on himself decreed. <br> But now come onward with thine eyes, as I <br> Speaking shall go, and note the great patricians <br> Of this most just and merciful of empires. <br> Those two that sit above there most enrapture <br> As being very near unto Augusta,<sup>2078</sup> <br> Are as it were the two roots of this Rose. <br> He who upon the left is near her placed<sup>2079</sup> <br> The father is, by whose audacious taste <br> The human species so much bitter tastes. <br> Upon the right thou seest that ancient father<sup>2080</sup> <br> Of Holy Church, into whose keeping Christ <br> The keys committed of this lovely flower. <br> And he who all the evil days beheld,<sup>2081</sup> <br> Before his death, of her the beauteous bride <br> Who with the spear and with the nails was won, <br> Beside him sits, and by the other rests <br> That leader under whom on manna lived<sup>2082</sup> <br> The people ingrate, fickle, and stiff-necked.<sup>2083</sup> <br> Opposite Peter seest thou Anna seated,<sup>2084</sup> <br> So well content to look upon her daughter, <br> Her eyes she moves not while she sings Hosanna. <br> And opposite the eldest household father <br> Lucia sits, she who thy Lady moved<sup>2085</sup> <br> When to rush downward thou didst bend thy brows. <br> But since the moments of thy vision fly, <br> Here will we make full stop, as a good tailor <br> Who makes the gown according to his cloth, <br> And unto the first Love will turn our eyes, <br> That looking upon Him thou penetrate <br> As far as possible through his effulgence. <br> Truly, lest peradventure thou recede, <br> Moving thy wings believing to advance,<sup>2086</sup> <br> By prayer behoves it that grace be obtained; <br> Grace from that one who has the power to aid thee; <br> And thou shalt follow me with thy affection <br> That from my words thy heart turn not aside.” <br> And he began this holy orison.</p>
