<p class="lede">The triumph of the Church.</p>
<p class="verse">Singing like unto an enamoured lady<sup>1083</sup> <br> She, with the ending of her words, continued: <br> “<i>Beati quorum tecta sunt peccata.</i>”<sup>1084</sup> <br> And even as Nymphs, that wandered all alone <br> Among the sylvan shadows, sedulous <br> One to avoid and one to see the sun, <br> She then against the stream moved onward, going <br> Along the bank, and I abreast of her, <br> Her little steps with little steps attending. <br> Between her steps and mine were not a hundred,<sup>1085</sup> <br> When equally the margins gave a turn, <br> In such a way, that to the East I faced. <br> Nor even thus our way continued far <br> Before the lady wholly turned herself <br> Unto me, saying, “Brother, look and listen!” <br> And lo! a sudden lustre ran across <br> On every side athwart the spacious forest, <br> Such that it made me doubt if it were lightning. <br> But since the lightning ceases as it comes, <br> And that continuing brightened more and more, <br> Within my thought I said, “What thing is this?” <br> And a delicious melody there ran <br> Along the luminous air, whence holy zeal <br> Made me rebuke the hardihood of Eve; <br> For there where earth and heaven obedient were, <br> The woman only, and but just created, <br> Could not endure to stay ’neath any veil; <br> Underneath which had she devoutly stayed, <br> I sooner should have tasted those delights <br> Ineffable, and for a longer time. <br> While ’mid such manifold first-fruits I walked <br> Of the eternal pleasure all enrapt, <br> And still solicitous of more delights, <br> In front of us like an enkindled fire <br> Became the air beneath the verdant boughs, <br> And the sweet sound as singing now was heard. <br> O Virgins sacrosanct! if ever hunger, <br> Vigils, or cold for you I have endured, <br> The occasion spurs me their reward to claim! <br> Now Helicon must needs pour forth for me, <br> And with her choir Urania must assist me,<sup>1086</sup> <br> To put in verse things difficult to think. <br> A little farther on, seven trees of gold <br> In semblance the long space still intervening <br> Between ourselves and them did counterfeit; <br> But when I had approached so near to them <br> The common object, which the sense deceives,<sup>1087</sup> <br> Lost not by distance any of its marks, <br> The faculty that lends discourse to reason<sup>1088</sup> <br> Did apprehend that they were candlesticks,<sup>1089</sup> <br> And in the voices of the song “Hosanna!” <br> Above them flamed the harness beautiful, <br> Far brighter than the moon in the serene <br> Of midnight, at the middle of her month. <br> I turned me round, with admiration filled, <br> To good Virgilius, and he answered me <br> With visage no less full of wonderment. <br> Then back I turned my face to those high things, <br> Which moved themselves towards us so sedately, <br> They had been distanced by new-wedded brides. <br> The lady chid me: “Why dost thou burn only <br> So with affection for the living lights, <br> And dost not look at what comes after them?” <br> Then saw I people, as behind their leaders, <br> Coming behind them, garmented in white, <br> And such a whiteness never was on earth. <br> The water on my left flank was resplendent, <br> And back to me reflected my left side, <br> E’en as a mirror, if I looked therein. <br> When I upon my margin had such post <br> That nothing but the stream divided us, <br> Better to see I gave my steps repose; <br> And I beheld the flamelets onward go, <br> Leaving behind themselves the air depicted, <br> And they of trailing pennons had the semblance, <br> So that it overhead remained distinct <br> With sevenfold lists, all of them of the colors <br> Whence the sun’s bow is made, and Delia’s girdle.<sup>1090</sup> <br> These standards to the rearward longer were <br> Than was my sight; and, as it seemed to me, <br> Ten paces were the outermost apart. <br> Under so fair a heaven as I describe <br> The four and twenty Elders, two by two,<sup>1091</sup> <br> Came on incoronate with flower-de-luce. <br> They all of them were singing: “Blessed thou<sup>1092</sup> <br> Among the daughters of Adam art, and blessed <br> For evermore shall be thy loveliness.” <br> After the flowers and other tender grasses <br> In front of me upon the other margin <br> Were disencumbered of that race elect, <br> Even as in heaven star followeth after star, <br> There came close after them four animals,<sup>1093</sup> <br> Incoronate each one with verdant leaf. <br> Plumed with six wings was every one of them, <br> The plumage full of eyes; the eyes of Argus <br> If they were living would be such as these. <br> Reader! to trace their forms no more I waste <br> My rhymes; for other spendings press me so, <br> That I in this cannot be prodigal. <br> But read Ezekiel, who depicteth them<sup>1094</sup> <br> As he beheld them from the region cold <br> Coming with cloud, with whirlwind, and with fire; <br> And such as thou shalt find them in his pages, <br> Such were they here; saving that in their plumage <br> John is with me, and differeth from him.<sup>1095</sup> <br> The interval between these four contained <br> A chariot triumphal on two wheels,<sup>1096</sup> <br> Which by a Griffin’s neck came drawn along;<sup>1097</sup> <br> And upward he extended both his wings <br> Between the middle list and three and three,<sup>1098</sup> <br> So that he injured none by cleaving it. <br> So high they rose that they were lost to sight; <br> His limbs were gold, so far as he was bird, <br> And white the others with vermilion mingled. <br> Not only Rome with no such splendid car <br> E’er gladdened Africanus, or Augustus, <br> But poor to it that of the Sun would be⁠—<sup>1099</sup> <br> That of the Sun, which swerving was burnt up <br> At the importunate orison of Earth, <br> When Jove was so mysteriously just.<sup>1100</sup> <br> Three maidens at the right wheel in a circle<sup>1101</sup> <br> Came onward dancing; one so very red <br> That in the fire she hardly had been noted. <br> The second was as if her flesh and bones <br> Had all been fashioned out of emerald; <br> The third appeared as snow but newly fallen. <br> And now they seemed conducted by the white, <br> Now by the red, and from the song of her <br> The others took their step, or slow or swift. <br> Upon the left hand four made holiday<sup>1102</sup> <br> Vested in purple, following the measure <br> Of one of them with three eyes in her head. <br> In rear of all the group here treated of<sup>1103</sup> <br> Two old men I beheld, unlike in habit, <br> But like in gait, each dignified and grave. <br> One showed himself as one of the disciples<sup>1104</sup> <br> Of that supreme Hippocrates, whom nature <br> Made for the animals she holds most dear; <br> Contrary care the other manifested, <br> With sword so shining and so sharp, it caused<sup>1105</sup> <br> Terror to me on this side of the river. <br> Thereafter four I saw of humble aspect,<sup>1106</sup> <br> And behind all an aged man alone<sup>1107</sup> <br> Walking in sleep with countenance acute. <br> And like the foremost company these seven <br> Were habited; yet of the flower-de-luce <br> No garland round about the head they wore, <br> But of the rose, and other flowers vermilion; <br> At little distance would the sight have sworn <br> That all were in a flame above their brows. <br> And when the car was opposite to me <br> Thunder was heard; and all that folk august <br> Seemed to have further progress interdicted, <br> There with the vanward ensigns standing still.<sup>1108</sup></p>
