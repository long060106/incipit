<p class="lede">Beatrice’s discourse of the creation of the Angels, and of the fall of Lucifer⁠—Her reproof of the ignorance and avarice of preachers, and the sale of indulgences.</p>
<p class="verse">At what time both the children of Latona,<sup>1990</sup> <br> Surmounted by the Ram and by the Scales,<sup>1991</sup> <br> Together make a zone of the horizon,<sup>1992</sup> <br> As long as from the time the zenith holds them <br> In equipoise, till from that girdle both <br> Changing their hemisphere disturb the balance, <br> So long, her face depicted with a smile, <br> Did Beatrice keep silence while she gazed <br> Fixedly at the point which had o’ercome me.<sup>1993</sup> <br> Then she began: “I say, and I ask not <br> What thou dost wish to hear, for I have seen it<sup>1994</sup> <br> Where centres every When and every <i>Ubi</i>.<sup>1995</sup> <br> Not to acquire some good unto himself, <br> Which is impossible, but that his splendor<sup>1996</sup> <br> In its resplendency may say, ‘<i>Subsisto,</i>’ <br> In his eternity outside of time,<sup>1997</sup> <br> Outside all other limits, as it pleased him, <br> Into new Loves the Eternal Love unfolded.<sup>1998</sup> <br> Nor as if torpid did he lie before; <br> For neither after nor before proceeded <br> The going forth of God upon these waters.<sup>1999</sup> <br> Matter and Form unmingled and conjoined<sup>2000</sup> <br> Came into being that had no defect,<sup>2001</sup> <br> E’en as three arrows from a three-stringed bow. <br> And as in glass, in amber, or in crystal <br> A sunbeam flashes so, that from its coming <br> To its full being is no interval, <br> So from its Lord did the triform effect <br> Ray forth into its being all together, <br> Without discrimination of beginning. <br> Order was con-created and constructed <br> In substances, and summit of the world <br> Were those wherein the pure act was produced.<sup>2002</sup> <br> Pure potentiality held the lowest part;<sup>2003</sup> <br> Midway bound potentiality with act<sup>2004</sup> <br> Such bond that it shall never be unbound.<sup>2005</sup> <br> Jerome has written unto you of angels<sup>2006</sup> <br> Created a long lapse of centuries <br> Or ever yet the other world was made; <br> But written is this truth in many places<sup>2007</sup> <br> By writers of the Holy Ghost, and thou<sup>2008</sup> <br> Shalt see it, if thou lookest well thereat. <br> And even reason seeth it somewhat, <br> For it would not concede that for so long <br> Could be the motors without their perfection.<sup>2009</sup> <br> Now dost thou know both where and when these Loves <br> Created were, and how; so that extinct <br> In thy desire already are three fires. <br> Nor could one reach, in counting, unto twenty <br> So swiftly, as a portion of these angels <br> Disturbed the subject of your elements.<sup>2010</sup> <br> The rest remained, and they began this art <br> Which thou discernest, with so great delight <br> That never from their circling do they cease. <br> The occasion of the fall was the accursed <br> Presumption of that One, whom thou hast seen<sup>2011</sup> <br> By all the burden of the world constrained. <br> Those whom thou here beholdest modest were <br> To recognise themselves as of that goodness <br> Which made them apt for so much understanding; <br> On which account their vision was exalted <br> By the enlightening grace and their own merit, <br> So that they have a full and steadfast will. <br> I would not have thee doubt, but certain be, <br> ’Tis meritorious to receive this grace,<sup>2012</sup> <br> According as the affection opens to it. <br> Now round about in this consistory <br> Much mayst thou contemplate, if these my words <br> Be gathered up, without all further aid. <br> But since upon the earth, throughout your schools, <br> They teach that such is the angelic nature <br> That it doth hear, and recollect, and will, <br> More will I say, that thou mayst see unmixed <br> The truth that is confounded there below, <br> Equivocating in suchlike prelections. <br> These substances, since in God’s countenance <br> They jocund were, turned not away their sight <br> From that wherefrom not anything is hidden; <br> Hence they have not their vision intercepted <br> By object new, and hence they do not need <br> To recollect, through interrupted thought. <br> So that below, not sleeping, people dream, <br> Believing they speak truth, and not believing; <br> And in the last is greater sin and shame. <br> Below you do not journey by one path <br> Philosophising; so transporteth you <br> Love of appearance and the thought thereof. <br> And even this above here is endured <br> With less disdain, than when is set aside <br> The Holy Writ, or when it is distorted. <br> They think not there how much of blood it costs <br> To sow it in the world, and how he pleases <br> Who in humility keeps close to it. <br> Each striveth for appearance, and doth make <br> His own inventions; and these treated are<sup>2013</sup> <br> By preachers, and the Evangel holds its peace. <br> One sayeth that the moon did backward turn, <br> In the Passion of Christ, and interpose herself <br> So that the sunlight reached not down below; <br> And lies; for of its own accord the light <br> Hid itself; whence to Spaniards and to Indians, <br> As to the Jews, did such eclipse respond. <br> Florence has not so many Lapi and Bindi<sup>2014</sup> <br> As fables such as these, that every year <br> Are shouted from the pulpit back and forth, <br> In such wise that the lambs, who do not know, <br> Come back from pasture fed upon the wind,<sup>2015</sup> <br> And not to see the harm doth not excuse them. <br> Christ did not to his first disciples say, <br> ‘Go forth, and to the world preach idle tales,’ <br> But unto them a true foundation gave; <br> And this so loudly sounded from their lips, <br> That, in the warfare to enkindle Faith, <br> They made of the Evangel shields and lances. <br> Now men go forth with jests and drolleries<sup>2016</sup> <br> To preach, and if but well the people laugh, <br> The hood puffs out, and nothing more is asked. <br> But in the cowl there nestles such a bird,<sup>2017</sup> <br> That, if the common people were to see it, <br> They would perceive what pardons they confide in, <br> For which so great on earth has grown the folly, <br> That, without proof of any testimony, <br> To each indulgence they would flock together. <br> By this Saint Anthony his pig doth fatten,<sup>2018</sup> <br> And many others, who are worse than pigs, <br> Paying in money without mark of coinage.<sup>2019</sup> <br> But since we have digressed abundantly, <br> Turn back thine eyes forthwith to the right path, <br> So that the way be shortened with the time. <br> This nature doth so multiply itself<sup>2020</sup> <br> In numbers, that there never yet was speech <br> Nor mortal fancy that can go so far. <br> And if thou notest that which is revealed <br> By Daniel, thou wilt see that in his thousands<sup>2021</sup> <br> Number determinate is kept concealed. <br> The primal light, that all irradiates it,<sup>2022</sup> <br> By modes as many is received therein, <br> As are the splendors wherewith it is mated.<sup>2023</sup> <br> Hence, inasmuch as on the act conceptive <br> The affection followeth, of love the sweetness<sup>2024</sup> <br> Therein diversely fervid is or tepid. <br> The height behold now and the amplitude <br> Of the eternal power, since it hath made <br> Itself so many mirrors, where ’tis broken, <br> One in itself remaining as before.”</p>
