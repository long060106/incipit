<p class="lede">The Tenth Heaven, or Empyrean⁠—The River of Light⁠—The Two Courts of Heaven⁠—The White Rose of Paradise.</p>
<p class="verse">Perchance six thousand miles remote from us<sup>2025</sup> <br> Is glowing the sixth hour, and now this world<sup>2026</sup> <br> Inclines its shadow almost to a level, <br> When the mid-heaven begins to make itself <br> So deep to us, that here and there a star <br> Ceases to shine so far down as this depth, <br> And as advances bright exceedingly <br> The handmaid of the sun, the heaven is closed <br> Light after light to the most beautiful; <br> Not otherwise the Triumph, which forever<sup>2027</sup> <br> Plays round about the point that vanquished me, <br> Seeming enclosed by what itself encloses, <br> Little by little from my vision faded; <br> Whereat to turn mine eyes on Beatrice <br> My seeing nothing and my love constrained me. <br> If what has hitherto been said of her <br> Were all concluded in a single praise, <br> Scant would it be to serve the present turn. <br> Not only does the beauty I beheld <br> Transcend ourselves, but truly I believe <br> Its Maker only may enjoy it all. <br> Vanquished do I confess me by this passage <br> More than by problem of his theme was ever <br> O’ercome the comic or the tragic poet; <br> For as the sun the sight that trembles most, <br> Even so the memory of that sweet smile <br> My mind depriveth of its very self. <br> From the first day that I beheld her face <br> In this life, to the moment of this look, <br> The sequence of my song has ne’er been severed; <br> But now perforce this sequence must desist <br> From following her beauty with my verse, <br> As every artist at his uttermost. <br> Such as I leave her to a greater fame <br> Than any of my trumpet, which is bringing <br> Its arduous matter to a final close, <br> With voice and gesture of a perfect leader <br> She recommenced: “We from the greatest body<sup>2028</sup> <br> Have issued to the heaven that is pure light; <br> Light intellectual replete with love, <br> Love of true good replete with ecstasy, <br> Ecstasy that transcendeth every sweetness.<sup>2029</sup> <br> Here shalt thou see the one host and the other<sup>2030</sup> <br> Of Paradise, and one in the same aspects <br> Which at the final judgment thou shalt see.”<sup>2031</sup> <br> Even as a sudden lightning that disperses <br> The visual spirits, so that it deprives <br> The eye of impress from the strongest objects, <br> Thus round about me flashed a living light, <br> And left me swathed around with such a veil <br> Of its effulgence, that I nothing saw. <br> “Ever the Love which quieteth this heaven <br> Welcomes into itself with such salute, <br> To make the candle ready for its flame.” <br> No sooner had within me these brief words <br> An entrance found, than I perceived myself <br> To be uplifted over my own power, <br> And I with vision new rekindled me, <br> Such that no light whatever is so pure <br> But that mine eyes were fortified against it. <br> And light I saw in fashion of a river<sup>2032</sup> <br> Fulvid with its effulgence, ’twixt two banks <br> Depicted with an admirable Spring. <br> Out of this river issued living sparks,<sup>2033</sup> <br> And on all sides sank down into the flowers, <br> Like unto rubies that are set in gold;<sup>2034</sup> <br> And then, as if inebriate with the odors, <br> They plunged again into the wondrous torrent, <br> And as one entered issued forth another. <br> “The high desire, that now inflames and moves thee <br> To have intelligence of what thou seest, <br> Pleaseth me all the more, the more it swells. <br> But of this water it behoves thee drink <br> Before so great a thirst in thee be slaked.” <br> Thus said to me the sunshine of mine eyes; <br> And added: “The river and the topazes<sup>2035</sup> <br> Going in and out, and the laughing of the herbage, <br> Are of their truth foreshadowing prefaces; <br> Not that these things are difficult in themselves, <br> But the deficiency is on thy side, <br> For yet thou hast not vision so exalted.” <br> There is no babe that leaps so suddenly <br> With face towards the milk, if he awake <br> Much later than his usual custom is, <br> As I did, that I might make better mirrors <br> Still of mine eyes, down stooping to the wave <br> Which flows that we therein be better made. <br> And even as the penthouse of mine eyelids <br> Drank of it, it forthwith appeared to me <br> Out of its length to be transformed to round.<sup>2036</sup> <br> Then as a folk who have been under masks <br> Seem other than before, if they divest <br> The semblance not their own they disappeared in, <br> Thus into greater pomp were changed for me <br> The flowerets and the sparks, so that I saw <br> Both of the Courts of Heaven made manifest. <br> O splendor of God! by means of which I saw <br> The lofty triumph of the realm veracious, <br> Give me the power to say how it I saw!<sup>2037</sup> <br> There is a light above, which visible<sup>2038</sup> <br> Makes the Creator unto every creature, <br> Who only in beholding Him has peace, <br> And it expands itself in circular form <br> To such extent, that its circumference <br> Would be too large a girdle for the sun. <br> The semblance of it is all made of rays <br> Reflected from the top of Primal Motion, <br> Which takes therefrom vitality and power. <br> And as a hill in water at its base <br> Mirrors itself, as if to see its beauty <br> When affluent most in verdure and in flowers, <br> So, ranged aloft all round about the light, <br> Mirrored I saw in more ranks than a thousand <br> All who above there have from us returned. <br> And if the lowest row collect within it <br> So great a light, how vast the amplitude <br> Is of this Rose in its extremest leaves! <br> My vision in the vastness and the height <br> Lost not itself, but comprehended all <br> The quantity and quality of that gladness. <br> There near and far nor add nor take away; <br> For there where God immediately doth govern, <br> The natural law in naught is relevant. <br> Into the yellow of the Rose Eternal<sup>2039</sup> <br> That spreads, and multiplies, and breathes an odor <br> Of praise unto the ever-vernal Sun, <br> As one who silent is and fain would speak, <br> Me Beatrice drew on, and said: “Behold <br> Of the white stoles how vast the convent is!<sup>2040</sup> <br> Behold how vast the circuit of our city! <br> Behold our seats so filled to overflowing, <br> That here henceforward are few people wanting! <br> On that great throne whereon thine eyes are fixed <br> For the crown’s sake already placed upon it, <br> Before thou suppest at this wedding feast <br> Shall sit the soul (that is to be Augustus<sup>2041</sup> <br> On earth) of noble Henry, who shall come<sup>2042</sup> <br> To redress Italy ere she be ready. <br> Blind covetousness, that casts its spell upon you, <br> Has made you like unto the little child, <br> Who dies of hunger and drives off the nurse. <br> And in the sacred forum then shall be<sup>2043</sup> <br> A Prefect such, that openly or covert<sup>2044</sup> <br> On the same road he will not walk with him. <br> But long of God he will not be endured <br> In holy office; he shall be thrust down <br> Where Simon Magus is for his deserts,<sup>2045</sup> <br> And make him of Alagna lower go!”<sup>2046</sup></p>
