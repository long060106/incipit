<p class="lede">The River Eunoë.</p>
<p class="verse">“<i>Deus venerunt gentes</i>,” alternating<sup>1172</sup> <br> Now three, now four, melodious psalmody <br> The maidens in the midst of tears began; <br> And Beatrice, compassionate and sighing, <br> Listened to them with such a countenance, <br> That scarce more changed was Mary at the cross. <br> But when the other virgins place had given<sup>1173</sup> <br> For her to speak, uprisen to her feet <br> With color as of fire, she made response: <br> “<i>Modicum, et non videbitis me</i>;<sup>1174</sup> <br> <i>Et iterum</i>, my sisters predilect, <br> <i>Modicum, et vos videbitis me.</i>” <br> Then all the seven in front of her she placed; <br> And after her, by beckoning only, moved <br> Me and the lady and the sage who stayed.<sup>1175</sup> <br> So she moved onward; and I do not think <br> That her tenth step was placed upon the ground, <br> When with her eyes upon mine eyes she smote, <br> And with a tranquil aspect, “Come more quickly,” <br> To me she said, “that, if I speak with thee, <br> To listen to me thou mayst be well placed.” <br> As soon as I was with her as I should be, <br> She said to me: “Why, brother, dost thou not <br> Venture to question now, in coming with me?” <br> As unto those who are too reverential, <br> Speaking in presence of superiors, <br> Who drag no living utterance to their teeth,<sup>1176</sup> <br> It me befell, that without perfect sound <br> Began I: “My necessity, Madonna, <br> You know, and that which thereunto is good.” <br> And she to me: “Of fear and bashfulness <br> Henceforward I will have thee strip thyself, <br> So that thou speak no more as one who dreams. <br> Know that the vessel which the serpent broke<sup>1177</sup> <br> Was, and is not; but let him who is guilty <br> Think that God’s vengeance does not fear a sop.<sup>1178</sup> <br> Without an heir shall not forever be<sup>1179</sup> <br> The Eagle that left his plumes upon the car, <br> Whence it became a monster, then a prey; <br> For verily I see, and hence narrate it, <br> The stars already near to bring the time, <br> From every hindrance safe, and every bar, <br> Within which a Five-hundred, Ten, and Five,<sup>1180</sup> <br> One sent from God, shall slay the thievish woman <br> And that same giant who is sinning with her. <br> And peradventure my dark utterance, <br> Like Themis and the Sphinx, may less persuade thee,<sup>1181</sup> <br> Since, in their mode, it clouds the intellect; <br> But soon the facts shall be the Naiades<sup>1182</sup> <br> Who shall this difficult enigma solve, <br> Without destruction of the flocks and harvests. <br> Note thou; and even as by me are uttered <br> These words, so teach them unto those who live <br> That life which is a running unto death;<sup>1183</sup> <br> And bear in mind, whene’er thou writest them, <br> Not to conceal what thou hast seen the plant, <br> That twice already has been pillaged here.<sup>1184</sup> <br> Whoever pillages or shatters it, <br> With blasphemy of deed offendeth God, <br> Who made it holy for his use alone. <br> For biting that, in pain and in desire<sup>1185</sup> <br> Five thousand years and more the first-born soul <br> Craved Him, who punished in himself the bite. <br> Thy genius slumbers, if it deem it not <br> For special reason so pre-eminent <br> In height, and so inverted in its summit.<sup>1186</sup> <br> And if thy vain imaginings had not been <br> Water of Elsa round about thy mind,<sup>1187</sup> <br> And Pyramus to the mulberry, their pleasure,<sup>1188</sup> <br> Thou by so many circumstances only <br> The justice of the interdict of God <br> Morally in the tree wouldst recognize. <br> But since I see thee in thine intellect <br> Converted into stone and stained with sin, <br> So that the light of my discourse doth daze thee, <br> I will too, if not written, at least painted, <br> Thou bear it back within thee, for the reason <br> That cinct with palm the pilgrim’s staff is borne.”<sup>1189</sup> <br> And I: “As by a signet is the wax <br> Which does not change the figure stamped upon it, <br> My brain is now imprinted by yourself. <br> But wherefore so beyond my power of sight <br> Soars your desirable discourse, that aye <br> The more I strive, so much the more I lose it?” <br> “That thou mayst recognize,” she said, “the school<sup>1190</sup> <br> Which thou hast followed, and mayst see how far <br> Its doctrine follows after my discourse, <br> And mayst behold your path from the divine <br> Distant as far as separated is <br> From earth the heaven that highest hastens on.” <br> Whence her I answered: “I do not remember <br> That ever I estranged myself from you, <br> Nor have I conscience of it that reproves me.” <br> “And if thou art not able to remember,” <br> Smiling she answered, “recollect thee now <br> That thou this very day hast drunk of Lethe; <br> And if from smoke a fire may be inferred, <br> Such an oblivion clearly demonstrates <br> Some error in thy will elsewhere intent. <br> Truly from this time forward shall my words <br> Be naked, so far as it is befitting <br> To lay them open unto thy rude gaze.” <br> And more coruscant and with slower steps <br> The sun was holding the meridian circle,<sup>1191</sup> <br> Which, with the point of view, shifts here and there, <br> When halted (as he cometh to a halt, <br> Who goes before a squadron as its escort, <br> If something new he find upon his way) <br> The maidens seven at a dark shadow’s edge, <br> Such as, beneath green leaves and branches black, <br> The Alp upon its frigid border wears. <br> In front of them the Tigris and Euphrates<sup>1192</sup> <br> Methought I saw forth issue from one fountain, <br> And slowly part, like friends, from one another. <br> “O light, O glory of the human race! <br> What stream is this which here unfolds itself <br> From out one source, and from itself withdraws?” <br> For such a prayer, ’twas said unto me, “Pray <br> Matilda that she tell thee”; and here answered, <br> As one does who doth free himself from blame, <br> The beautiful lady: “This and other things <br> Were told to him by me; and sure I am <br> The water of Lethe has not hid them from him.” <br> And Beatrice: “Perhaps a greater care, <br> Which oftentimes our memory takes away, <br> Has made the vision of his mind obscure. <br> But Eunoë behold, that yonder rises;<sup>1193</sup> <br> Lead him to it, and, as thou art accustomed, <br> Revive again the half-dead virtue in him.”<sup>1194</sup> <br> Like gentle soul, that maketh no excuse, <br> But makes its own will of another’s will <br> As soon as by a sign it is disclosed, <br> Even so, when she had taken hold of me, <br> The beautiful lady moved, and unto Statius <br> Said, in her womanly manner, “Come with him.” <br> If, Reader, I possessed a longer space <br> For writing it, I yet would sing in part <br> Of the sweet draught that ne’er would satiate me; <br> But inasmuch as full are all the leaves <br> Made ready for this second canticle, <br> The curb of art no farther lets me go. <br> From the most holy water I returned <br> Regenerate, in the manner of new trees <br> That are renewed with a new foliage, <br> Pure and disposed to mount unto the stars.<sup>1195</sup></p>
