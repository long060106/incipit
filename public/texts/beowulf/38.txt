<p>The dragon spits fire.</p>
<p>The stranger began then to vomit forth fire,</p>
<p>To burn the great manor; the blaze then glimmered</p>
<p>For anguish to earlmen, not anything living</p>
<p>Was the hateful air-goer willing to leave there.</p>
<p>5</p>
<p>The war of the worm widely was noticed,</p>
<p>The feud of the foeman afar and anear,</p>
<p>How the enemy injured the earls of the Geatmen,</p>
<p>Harried with hatred: back he hied to the treasure,</p>
<p>To the well-hidden cavern ere the coming of daylight.</p>
<p>10</p>
<p>He had circled with fire the folk of those regions,</p>
<p>With brand and burning; in the barrow he trusted,</p>
<p>In the wall and his war-might: the weening deceived him.</p>
<p>Beowulf hears of the havoc wrought by the dragon.</p>
<p>Then straight was the horror to Beowulf published,</p>
<p>Early forsooth, that his own native homestead,<sup>1</sup></p>
<p>15</p>
<p>The best of buildings, was burning and melting,</p>
<p>Gift-seat of Geatmen. ’Twas a grief to the spirit</p>
<p>Of the good-mooded hero, the greatest of sorrows:</p>
<p>He fears that Heaven is punishing him for some crime.</p>
<p>The wise one weened then that wielding his kingdom</p>
<p>’Gainst the ancient commandments, he had bitterly angered</p>
<p>20</p>
<p>The Lord everlasting: with lorn meditations</p>
<p>His bosom welled inward, as was nowise his custom.</p>
<p>The fire-spewing dragon fully had wasted</p>
<p>The fastness of warriors, the water-land outward,</p>
<p>The manor with fire. The folk-ruling hero,</p>
<p>25</p>
<p>Prince of the Weders, was planning to wreak him.</p>
<p>The warmen’s defender bade them to make him,</p>
<p>Earlmen’s atheling, an excellent war-shield</p>
<p>He orders an iron shield to be made from him, wood is useless.</p>
<p>Wholly of iron: fully he knew then</p>
<p>That wood from the forest was helpless to aid him,</p>
<p>30</p>
<p>Shield against fire. The long-worthy ruler</p>
<p>Must live the last of his limited earth-days,</p>
<p>Of life in the world and the worm along with him,</p>
<p>Though he long had been holding hoard-wealth in plenty.</p>
<p>He determines to fight alone.</p>
<p>Then the ring-prince disdained to seek with a war-band,</p>
<p>35</p>
<p>With army extensive, the air-going ranger;</p>
<p>He felt no fear of the foeman’s assaults and</p>
<p>He counted for little the might of the dragon,</p>
<p>His power and prowess: for previously dared he</p>
<p>Beowulf’s early triumphs referred to</p>
<p>A heap of hostility, hazarded dangers,</p>
<p>40</p>
<p>War-thane, when Hrothgar’s palace he cleansèd,</p>
<p>Conquering combatant, clutched in the battle</p>
<p>The kinsmen of Grendel, of kindred detested.<sup>2</sup></p>
<p>Higelac’s death recalled.</p>
<p>’Twas of hand-fights not least where Higelac was slaughtered,</p>
<p>When the king of the Geatmen with clashings of battle,</p>
<p>45</p>
<p>Friend-lord of folks in Frisian dominions,</p>
<p>Offspring of Hrethrel perished through sword-drink,</p>
<p>With battle-swords beaten; thence Beowulf came then</p>
<p>On self-help relying, swam through the waters;</p>
<p>He bare on his arm, lone-going, thirty</p>
<p>50</p>
<p>Outfits of armor, when the ocean he mounted.</p>
<p>The Hetwars by no means had need to be boastful</p>
<p>Of their fighting afoot, who forward to meet him</p>
<p>Carried their war-shields: not many returned from</p>
<p>The brave-mooded battle-knight back to their homesteads.</p>

<p>Ecgtheow’s bairn o’er the bight-courses swam then,</p>
<p>Lone-goer lorn to his land-folk returning,</p>
<p>Where Hygd to him tendered treasure and kingdom,</p>
<p>Heardred’s lack of capacity to rule.</p>
<p>Rings and dominion: her son she not trusted,</p>
<p>To be able to keep the kingdom devised him</p>
<p>60</p>
<p>’Gainst alien races, on the death of King Higelac.</p>
<p>Beowulf’s tact and delicacy recalled.</p>
<p>Yet the sad ones succeeded not in persuading the atheling</p>
<p>In any way ever, to act as a suzerain</p>
<p>To Heardred, or promise to govern the kingdom;</p>
<p>Yet with friendly counsel in the folk he sustained him,</p>
<p>65</p>
