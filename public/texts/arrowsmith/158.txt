<p>Thus Martin came to his experiment. Stokes was appointed Surgeon General, vice Inchcape Jones, and he made an illegal assignment of Martin to St. Swithin’s Parish, as medical officer with complete power. This, and the concurrence of Cecil Twyford, made his experiment possible.</p>
<p>He was invited to stay at Twyford’s. His only trouble was the guarding of Leora. He did not know what he would encounter in St. Swithin’s, while Penrith Lodge was as safe as any place on the island. When Leora insisted that, during his experiment, the cold thing which had stilled the laughter of Sondelius might come to him and he might need her, he tried to satisfy her by promising that if there was a place for her in St. Swithin’s, he would send for her.</p>
<p>Naturally, he was lying.</p>
<p>“Hard enough to see Gustaf go. By thunder she’s not going to run risks!” he vowed.</p>
<p>He left her, protected by the maids and the soldier butler, with Dr. Oliver Marchand to look in when he could.</p>
